03

Where the edge actually is

From the deep-dive research pass — 25 claims through 3-vote adversarial verification, 10 survived. These are the lanes the measurement sprint (Gate 3) exists to confirm or kill. Each is a hypothesis to falsify with your own net-of-fee data, not a promise.

A

Kalshi favorite-longshot bias — and it doesn't shrink with scale

Verified

Present in nearly every category, weakest only in politics/entertainment. Critically, it does not get arbed away by big money — the largest-trade-size quintile shows the biggest bias in the data (300k+ transactions). That contradicts the usual "institutions correct it" assumption. The catch: the academic finding is pre-fee.

Falsify: the edge vanishes once Kalshi's live 2026 fees and the tighter-bias categories are netted out.
B

Kalshi single-name maker positioning — be the counterparty

Verified

Makers in single-name markets earn ~2× per contract (1.91¢ vs 0.82¢) — not from wider spreads, but because retail overbuys YES (~61% of buys) in markets that resolve YES only ~33% of the time. The edge is absorbing that behavioral surplus as the resting counterparty (41.6M trades, Stanford/Cornell).

Falsify: your own fills don't reproduce ~1.91¢/contract once your adverse-selection exposure — not the published aggregate — is measured.
C

PMUS "Accelerated Tier Placement" — a rebate side-door

Needs live check

Verbatim on the live fee page: you can be placed into PMUS's taker-rebate tiers (10/25/50%) by proving trailing-30-day volume on another venue — instead of grinding $250k/mo on PMUS first. If Polymarket-global volume qualifies, it changes the effective cost of trading at your bankroll.

Falsify: the mechanism doesn't apply cross-venue in practice, or the threshold is unreachable regardless of routing.
Ruled out — don't waste the sprint here
  • Naked sum-to-one / negRisk arb — bot-drained to ~$770/mo of scraps across a whole month of NBA markets.
  • Kalshi Fed-funds / macro-print markets — Susquehanna runs an institutional desk there, ~100M contracts/meeting. Not a niche.
  • Naive whale-copying, naive reward farming, LLM forecasting — coin-flip, adverse-selected, and no verified alpha respectively.
  • Public top-1%-wallet datasets — the candidate dataset's claims failed verification; this only closes with your own recorded data.
non-negotiable — these hold at every gate

Hard rules

  • Strategies emit opportunities only — only ExecutionEngine ever touches an order endpoint.
  • Every order passes RiskManager.check_order — fail-closed.
  • dry_run is the default everywhere; live is never the default of anything.
  • Cross-venue pairs trade only with resolution_verified: true — a human diffs the wording first.
  • Fees from core.fees in every edge calc — the zero-fee era is over.
  • Secrets only in .env. Never log a key or signature.
  • US-legal rails only — Polymarket global is read-only data. No VPNs, no geo-workarounds.
  • Measurement before money — paper telemetry gates live capital.
